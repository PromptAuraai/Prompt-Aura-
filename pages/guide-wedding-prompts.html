1. Introduction

Wedding photography is about capturing love, joy, and beautiful moments. AI prompts for weddings should be romantic, elegant, and emotionally resonant. This guide will help you create stunning wedding imagery with AI.

2. Bridal Portraits

Bridal portraits focus on the bride in her wedding dress. Key elements include:

  • Dress Details: Lace, train, veil, embroidery
  • Expression: Joyful, emotional, elegant
  • Lighting: Soft, romantic, golden hour
  • Background: Garden, church, studio, nature

3. Couple Photography

Couple photos capture the connection between two people. Focus on:

  • Intimacy: Natural poses, genuine connection
  • Setting: Scenic locations, meaningful places
  • Emotion: Love, joy, tenderness
  • Style: Candid, posed, editorial

💕 Pro Tip: Use keywords like "romantic," "elegant," "golden hour," and "dreamy" to create the right mood for wedding images.

4. Ceremony & Reception

Capture the key moments of the wedding day:

  • Ceremony: Vows, rings, first kiss
  • Reception: First dance, cake cutting, toasts
  • Guests: Candid moments, celebrations
  • Decorations: Flowers, lighting, table settings

5. Example Wedding Prompts

Bridal Portrait: "Elegant bride in a flowing white wedding dress with intricate lace details, long veil, golden hour garden setting, soft natural lighting, romantic atmosphere, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, dreamy bokeh background, high quality, photorealistic"
Couple Portrait: "Romantic couple embracing in a beautiful sunset, golden hour lighting, warm tones, dreamy atmosphere, wedding attire, natural pose, emotional connection, shallow depth of field, 50mm lens, cinematic style"
Wedding Ceremony: "Beautiful wedding ceremony in a sunlit church, bride and groom exchanging vows, soft natural light through stained glass windows, emotional moment, guests watching, elegant atmosphere, wide shot, cinematic composition"
